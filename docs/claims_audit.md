# Claims audit

## Strong (supported by aggregate results)
- The RR-feature PCG classifier can achieve good AF/SR discrimination *when it produces an AF/SR classification* (AF/SR-classified subset).
- Coverage is a major bottleneck for the RR-feature approach because it depends on annotated clean segments with sufficient S1/S2 cycles.

## Cautious
- Head-to-head comparisons with comparator devices should be framed carefully because coverage differs between methods.
- Reported performance is internal (OOF) and may not generalize to new cohorts.

## Must not claim
- PCG replaces 12-lead ECG.
- PCG is clinically equivalent to comparator devices.
- The current system is fully automated end-to-end (annotation dependence).
